# Future ideas — retention & depth

Backlog for possible features. Not committed work; use this to track what we might add later.

## Progression & “one more run”

- [ ] **Persistent meta-currency** (may already have scrap): tie clear goals to it—new ships, starting perks, cosmetic trails, or a small permanent stat (e.g. +1% pickup radius) with diminishing returns so it never trivializes difficulty.
- [ ] **Daily / weekly challenge** with fixed seed or modifier (“Thermal only”, “2× asteroids”) and a small reward—gives a reason to open the game even when not chasing a full run.
- [ ] **Milestone rewards** beyond achievements: titles, borders on the HUD, or ship skins at wave 10 / 20 / boss clears.

## Clarity of goals during a run

- [ ] **Run objectives panel**: “Reach wave 15”, “Beat boss without damage”, “Collect X scrap this run”—rotating 2–3 so players always have a sub-goal besides survival.
- [ ] **Post-run recap**: time survived, waves, damage taken, asteroids destroyed, best combo.

## Variety & mastery

- [ ] **Unlockable modifiers (mutators)**: start with 2–3 hard/easy toggles; players grind to unlock weirder ones—adds content without new art.
- [ ] **Weapon / card mastery**: small XP per run per card that unlocks alt fire or a gold border—gives reason to try different builds.

## Habit & comfort

- [ ] **Session length options**: “Quick run” (15 min cap) vs “Marathon”—retention often comes from matching real life, not only depth.
- [ ] **Cloud save / export code** (if multi-device): reduces fear of losing progress.

## Content & hazards

- [X] **Boss improvements**: Add more boss types and create specific behavior for each type.
- [X] **More asteroid types**: Create more asteroid types with different characteristics—e.g. ice asteroids that freeze you on touch, obsidian asteroids that require more shots to destroy and do more damage.
- [ ] **Environment traps**: e.g. lasers on the edge of the screen that turn on randomly.
